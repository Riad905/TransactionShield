# Temporal stability and evaluation contract

## Reproducible step evidence

[`transfer-step-summary.csv`](transfer-step-summary.csv) contains one deterministic row for every observed step with eligible-transfer count, fraud count, legitimate count, and fraud rate. It is generated by `transactionshield.contracts` from the ignored canonical raw CSV.

All 144 steps contain both eligible transfers and fraud. Per-step eligible counts range from 2,402 to 4,896 (median 3,930), and per-step fraud counts range from 1,015 to 1,326 (median 1,224). No step is sparse for count-based evaluation.

Individual step rates range from 24.591503% to 49.804382%, driven mainly by changing eligible counts while fraud counts are comparatively stable. A random split would average away this temporal variation and produce an easier, less honest estimate.

## Frozen chronological boundaries

The approved Stage 3 split is:

| Partition | Steps | Duration | Eligible transfers | Fraud | Legitimate | Fraud rate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Train | 0–95 | 96 steps | 375,732 | 117,441 | 258,291 | 31.256587% |
| Validation | 96–119 | 24 steps | 94,503 | 28,973 | 65,530 | 30.658286% |
| Test | 120–143 | 24 steps | 99,093 | 29,104 | 69,989 | 29.370389% |

This 96/24/24-step design gives the final two windows equal 24-step durations, keeps the latest complete window for final testing, and leaves tens of thousands of fraud examples in each holdout. It also exposes the model to a lower-prevalence final period rather than stratifying that shift away. These boundaries are frozen; rolling-cutoff sensitivity checks may supplement, but not replace, the untouched final window.

The artifact spans only six 24-step periods rather than the paper's reported 720-step configuration. Therefore this split cannot support claims about monthly seasonality or long-term drift.

## Evaluation contract

- The headline design is a chronological split on complete, non-overlapping step ranges.
- Random train/test splitting is prohibited as the headline result.
- Feature state for a transaction at step `t` may use unlabelled activity from every completed step with `step < t`, including completed earlier validation or test steps during chronological replay.
- Current-step transactions, future transactions, and validation/test fraud labels are forbidden from historical feature computation.
- All learned preprocessing parameters and model parameters must be fitted from the training partition only.
- Model selection may compare training-fitted candidates on validation data; threshold selection must use validation data only.
- Final test labels must remain untouched until the single final evaluation, and no threshold or preprocessing decision may use the test partition.

Primary evaluation:

- PR-AUC/Average Precision for threshold-independent minority-class ranking;
- precision and recall at explicitly selected operating points;
- confusion-matrix counts at those operating points; and
- calibration assessment using reliability summaries and Brier score if outputs are described as probabilities.

Secondary evaluation:

- F1 at the declared threshold;
- ROC-AUC as context, never the sole headline metric;
- alert rate and predicted-positive count to make operational load visible; and
- slice results by step band and available history depth.

The transfer subset has moderate class imbalance: 30.828977% fraud and 69.171023% legitimate. Accuracy is not the headline metric because operational fraud decisions have asymmetric false-positive and false-negative consequences, and useful performance depends on the threshold-specific precision, recall, alert-volume, and intervention trade-offs. Accuracy alone does not express those consequences.

Probability estimation and threshold selection are separate contracts. A training-fitted model estimates or ranks risk; an operational threshold converts that score into an action under review-capacity, precision, recall, and false-positive trade-offs. Thresholds must be selected using validation data only and declared scenarios, then frozen before the final test evaluation.

## Stage 4C continuity check

The implemented history engine does not receive a partition label and has no
partition-reset operation. Its only temporal boundary is completion of a whole
`step`. Validation acceptance counts apply the frozen windows to emitted transfer
metadata outside the predictor object. Tests verify continuity at 95 to 96 and
119 to 120, including unlabelled activity from earlier holdout steps. Stage 4C
does not fit preprocessing/models, tune thresholds, evaluate fraud performance,
or create final train/validation/test feature files.
